# Research to Experiment handoff

Research closes when a decision-grade repository package records the question,
source map, supporting and disconfirming evidence, findings, limitations,
disposition, implementing links, and review trigger.

Measured uncertainty continues in a separate Experiment. Its issue records:

- Decision question and hypothesis.
- Protocol version and acceptance rule selected before results.
- Implementation and comparator revisions.
- Workload, environment, exclusions, and raw artifact identity.
- Correctness admission and invalidation rules.
- Samples, uncertainty, effect size, and conclusion.
- Evidence-level and claim-state consequence.
- Follow-up implementation or requalification work.

Do not keep narrative Research open merely because reproduction is pending. Do
not close an implicit Experiment by citing literature or a source-code study.
Research can justify E2; Alpine-controlled reproduction is E3; scoped dominance
requires E4.
