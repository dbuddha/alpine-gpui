Alpine is an independent narrow implementation informed by selected GPUI and
Zed patterns. It does not ship GPUI, WGPU, or Zed application code.

The canonical lineage package records:

- Exact Alpine, Zed, WGPU, awesome-gpui, and lab revisions.
- Adapted concepts, independent convergence, Alpine-original strengthening,
  comparator-only inputs, rejected scope, and deferred work.
- Direct Alpine GPUI versus Zed GPUI and Alpine Studio versus Zed Editor matrices.
- Per-mechanism correctness, performance, and memory evidence levels.
- Append-only implementation and supersession history.
- Adversarial findings and the missing E3/E4 experiment queue.

Current claim boundary: deterministic bounded-resource and avoided-work
invariants exist, but comparative GPUI, Zed, and Sublime dominance is not yet
qualified. Visible release typing latency remains a critical blocker. The
realistic prepared-scene ladder and six-step atlas lifecycle have composed E3
semantic admission. Timing, adaptation, renderer residency, and independent
E4 comparison remain open through #470, #471, and #472.
