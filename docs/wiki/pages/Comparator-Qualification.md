# Comparator and qualification protocol

> Retrieval mirror synchronized from Alpine `main` revision
> `{{ALPINE_MAIN_REVISION}}`. The repository mdBook is canonical.

Alpine reports renderer-only, adaptation-only, framework, normalized-product,
and stock-product measurements separately. Every claim pins code, workload,
hardware, OS, display, power, thermal state, fonts, settings, and tool versions.

Correctness, lifecycle, accessibility, and residency invariants must be green
before a performance claim is eligible. Raw paired samples, randomized AB/BA
order, cold and warm separation, percentiles, effect sizes, confidence
intervals, and explicit invalidation reasons are retained. No universal
framework claim is inferred from editor-only evidence.

Local Studio sessions first enter the revision-bound dogfood capture protocol.
Its sealed stage, resource, process-memory, accessibility, lifecycle, and
shutdown records make no performance claim. Synthetic fixtures prove only the
schema; automatic Studio emission and physical baselines remain tracked work.
